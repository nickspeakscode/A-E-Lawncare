FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Call (864) 473-9976 or email support@aelawnpros.com.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homes and businesses across Upstate South Carolina and nearby Western North Carolina, centered on Spartanburg, Inman and Landrum, SC and Tryon and Columbus, NC. If you are close to these towns, call us and we will confirm availability for your property.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Send a quote request online or call us, and we will follow up with a clear, honest estimate for the work.

Do you work on residential and commercial properties?

Both. We handle recurring maintenance for residential and commercial properties, as well as one-time projects like hardscapes, retaining walls, sod and grading.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. A&E Lawn Care crews are licensed and insured.

What services do you offer?

Lawn mowing and maintenance, sod installation, paver walkways and staircases, retaining walls, grading and drainage, fence building and repair (including horse fences), planting beds, raised beds, mulch and pine needles, leaf and debris cleanup, shrub trimming, tree and stump removal, and mini excavator and skid steer work.

Can you bundle several jobs together?

Yes. Many customers combine projects, for example a cleanup, mulch and a new walkway, into one plan so the work is scheduled together and finished cleanly.
